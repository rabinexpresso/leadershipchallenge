How to Set Up?

Three steps. The host runs from a laptop — players join on their own phones.

Step 1 · Host
Open the app on a laptop
Tap Host Multiplayer on the home screen. A room code and QR code appear on screen — you're ready.
Step 2 · Host
Project onto a TV or screen
Mirror or cast your laptop to a shared display so the whole room can see the QR code and game as it runs.
Step 3 · Players
Scan the QR or enter the room code
Players open the app on their own phone and scan the QR on screen — or tap Join a Game and type the room code. When everyone's in, the host taps Start Game.
02

What is this challenge?

You'll face up to 15 real leadership situations drawn directly from conversations with leaders at HLE/Alaya — the exact problems they described facing day-to-day. You choose how many to play when you start.

For each situation, you'll pick how you'd respond. After everyone picks, you'll see what actually happens as a result — and why.

At the end, you'll get a Leadership Profile showing your strengths and where to grow next.

Playing with a group? The host runs the app on a laptop projected onto a screen. Each player joins on their own phone and picks simultaneously. Everyone's choice stays hidden until the host reveals them together.

04

The Framework
Behind the Scores

Trust
Based on Patrick Lencioni's Five Dysfunctions of a Team and Brené Brown's vulnerability-based trust research. The question: does your team feel safe enough to be honest with you?
Proactivity
Covey's "Be Proactive" habit combined with Goleman's Emotional Intelligence research — specifically social awareness: noticing what's happening in your team before anyone says it out loud.
Empowerment
Hersey & Blanchard's Situational Leadership model. Real delegation means transferring the why, not just the what — and trusting people to make it better than you would have.
Adaptability
John Kotter's change leadership model and McKinsey's adaptive leadership research. The best leaders don't just survive change — they help their team move through it with momentum.

No "perfect" score exists. Every scenario rewards different dimensions differently — and your maximum possible score scales with game length. Real leadership always involves tradeoffs — that's the point.

05

How each round works

Every scenario follows the same four-step flow:

Step 1 — The clock is ticking
A 2-minute timer starts the moment a scenario loads. It's not enforced, but it keeps the energy honest — don't overthink it.
Step 2 — Each player picks privately
Everyone picks on their own phone simultaneously. No peeking at what others chose — picks stay hidden until the host reveals them.
Step 3 — Reveal all at once
The host hits Reveal. Seeing where instincts differ — or align — is where the real conversation begins.
Step 4 — Read the outcome, then next
Each choice has a real consequence explained in full. Don't skip it — it's where the learning happens. Then straight to the next scenario.
